// faq

Questions, answered

What kind of projects do you work on?

Business websites, landing pages, booking systems, admin dashboards and custom web apps. If it runs in a browser, we can likely build it.

Can you build a website for a small business?

Yes, that is our most common project. We design for phones first, because most of your customers will visit from one.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the form below or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us what the business does and what you want the site to do. We reply with scope and price.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We handle content updates, bug fixes and new features after your project goes live.
